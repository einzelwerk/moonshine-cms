# Changelog

All notable changes to `ewk/moonshine-cms` are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Until
`1.0.0` a minor version may contain breaking changes.

## 0.2.4 — 2026-10-30

### Added

- `ProvidesSeoDescriptionFallback`: an owner that implements it answers an empty
  description with a text of its own. The document strips tags and whitespace runs and
  cuts the text at a word to 155 characters with an ellipsis; Open Graph and Twitter
  take it from the plain description. `ProvidesSeoFallbacks` is unchanged.

## 0.2.3 — 2026-10-30

### Fixed

- The canonical address and the language versions keep the page number of numbered
  pagination only: the growing slice of a "show more" list (`b[<anchor>][shown]`) and
  a portion size the visitor asked for (`b[<anchor>][per_page]`) are dropped like the
  filters, so a longer slice no longer gets indexed as a page of its own.

### Added

- `ResolvedParameters::sliceQueryKeys()` and the optional `SeoContext::$sliceKeys`.

## 0.2.2 — 2026-10-25

### Breaking

- `CmsResolveResponse.subject` refers to the new component `CmsResolveSubject`
  (`BlockSubject` plus `slug`) as `type: object, nullable: true, allOf: [$ref]`. The wire
  is unchanged; a generated client gets `CmsResolveSubject | null` and loses the inline
  `CmsResolveResponseSubject` type.

### Fixed

- `variant` of a block envelope is `nullable: true` with the enum of the declared
  variants; `null` is no longer listed inside the `enum`.
- The operations of the package (`CmsChromeV1`, `CmsMenuV1`, `CmsResolveV1`,
  `CmsShowBlockV1`, `CmsSitemapIndexV1`, `CmsSitemapFileV1`) declare `security: []`.

### Added

- `PageSecurityProcessor` and `cms-pages.openapi.security` (default `[]`): the security
  requirements of `CmsResolveV1` and `CmsShowBlockV1`, whose blocks may tailor the
  answer to the visitor. An installation lists the identity as optional in its own
  scheme names, `[[], ['clientUuid' => []]]`, and registers the processor in
  `l5-swagger.scanOptions.processors`; a scheme the document does not declare is a
  `LogicException`.

### Changed

- Every reference of the contract carries a description, wrapped into `allOf`: `data`
  and `children` of the block envelopes, `pagination` and `filters` of a payload (also
  through `BlockSchemaGenerator::paginationProperty()`), `data` of the subject variants,
  `CmsBlockResponse.data` and `data` of the chrome, menu, resolver and sitemap answers.
  `CmsMetadata.openGraph` is described in Russian.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-10-25

### Fixed

- `subject.data` of a template type without a presenter leaves as `{}` instead of `[]`,
  from the resolver and from `SubjectPresentation::payload()`; the subject itself stays,
  so the client still narrows by `type`. The `BlockSubject<Studly>` variant of such a
  type describes `data` as an object without keys, and `CmsSubjectData` lists it so.
- `ChromePart.content` of a header or a footer without declared fields leaves as `{}`.

### Changed

- Every property generated from a declared field carries a description: the help, else
  the label, followed by the note of its format (`HTML на языке ответа`); a field with
  neither is described as `Поле <name>`.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-25

### Breaking

- `BlockSubject` is generated by the new `Ewk\Cms\Pages\OpenApi\SubjectSchemasProcessor`
  out of the page types: a `oneOf` of `BlockSubject<Studly>` variants
  `{type: enum[key], data}` with a discriminator on `type`. Register the processor in
  `l5-swagger.scanOptions.processors`; an installation that declares `BlockSubject`
  itself must drop it, a different shape is a `LogicException`. A presenter names the
  component of its `data` by implementing `DescribesSubjectSchema`; `CmsSubjectData`
  becomes the `anyOf` of those components unless the installation declares it.
  `CommonSchemas::SUBJECT_DATA` is gone.
- `CmsResolveResponse.subject` is `BlockSubject` plus `slug`, so it narrows by `type`.
- Empty dictionaries leave as `{}` instead of `[]`: `filters.fields` of a block without
  filters and `action.params` of an action without parameters.
  `FiltersPayload::make()` returns `fields` as `stdClass` when empty.
- Every refusal carries a `code`: `bad_request` for a language prefix, `not_found` for
  every 404 of the resolver, the block endpoint, the menus and the sitemap.
  `CmsError.code` (`bad_request`, `not_found`, `too_many_requests`) and
  `CmsValidationError.code` (`validation_failed`) are required; the installation's
  exception handler must send them on 422 and 429.

### Changed

- `CmsLink.action` is described as `{name: string, params: object}`.
- Nullable references (`ChromeMenuItem.link`, `CmsResolveResponse.metadata`, generated
  media and link fields) declare `type: object`.
- Short Russian descriptions on every operation, parameter and property of the
  contract; `x-cms-params` is unchanged.
- The component merging of the processors lives in `ComponentMerger`.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-16

First public release: a block CMS for Laravel 13 with an admin for MoonShine 4,
built on `ewk/moonshine-media` for files, pictures and content languages.

### Blocks (`Ewk\Cms\Blocks`)

- Block types declared by one schema each, from which the admin form, the
  validation rules, the stored shape, the payload and the OpenAPI description
  are derived; a neutral field dictionary with translatable values.
- One row per block with nesting in named slots of containers, checked on every
  write; shared blocks inserted on many pages and refused deletion while in
  use.
- Drafts and publication: the live rows are the draft, `PublishOwner` writes
  the snapshot the public answer reads and refuses with the list of what stands
  in its way.
- Typed links stored by kind and identifiers and resolved on render, with a
  registry of resolvers and of actions.
- Dynamic blocks with declared parameters, three pagination modes, filters and
  ready addresses; one query encoder for every address of the CMS.
- A swagger-php processor that generates the contract of the registered block
  types.
- Usages of media files registered for every block, with the owner named in the
  list of «where this file is used».

### Pages (`Ewk\Cms\Pages`)

- The tree of pages with derived paths, strict slugs and reserved segments.
- Page types declared in code: free pages, singletons and templates rendered
  for the entities of a model at the address of a path template.
- `GET /resolve` and `GET /blocks/{uuid}`, with contributors that extend the
  answer, and link resolvers for pages, catalogues with filters, entities and
  routes of the frontend.

### Layouts (`Ewk\Cms\Layouts`)

- Layouts declared by the application, headers and footers with declared
  fields, menus put into a closed dictionary of slots, places for menus on
  records of the application.
- `GET /chrome` and `GET /menus/{code}`, the same for every visitor; the layout
  of a page in the answer of the resolver.

### SEO (`Ewk\Cms\Seo`)

- Hand-filled metadata per record with fallbacks computed when the document is
  handed out, in the shape of `Metadata` of Next: canonical addresses,
  language versions, Open Graph and Twitter cards, robots.
- A registry of JSON-LD builders and the sitemap feed (`GET /sitemap`,
  `GET /sitemap/{id}`) derived from the page types.
- The picture of a card is an absolute address even when the disk of the media
  library answers with a bare path: such a path is completed with `app.url`,
  and an absolute address passes through unchanged.

### Admin (`Ewk\MoonShineCms`)

- The block editor with a panel of publication, one language switch, cards
  sorted by drag and drop across slots, and an insertion library narrowed per
  point of insertion.
- Sections of pages (a tree with templates as tiles), shared blocks, layouts,
  headers, footers and menus (a tree of items), with entity forms in one
  language scope.
- The link picker with its lookups inside the panel, and the metadata tab with
  a snippet preview and counters.
- Usage links and captions registered in the `UsageLinkRegistry` of the media
  admin; the «Медиа» section placed in `MoonShineCms::menuItems()`.
- Russian and English translations.
